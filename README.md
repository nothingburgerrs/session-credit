# PhoneKeys

Use your Android phone as a **Bluetooth keyboard** for your Samsung tablet. Each keypress plays a typing sound.

The tablet sees the phone as a normal Bluetooth keyboard, so typing works in **every app**: Notes, Chrome, Samsung Notes, messages, games and so on. Nothing needs to be installed on the tablet.

## Features
- Type with your phone's own keyboard (Samsung Keyboard, Gboard, etc.). Autocorrect and swipe typing work too.
- Typing sounds on the phone: **Mechanical, Typewriter (with a bell on Enter), Soft, Bubble, Clicky**, or Off. You can change the volume.
- Optional vibration on each key.
- Extra keys: Esc, Tab, arrow keys, Backspace, Delete, Enter.
- Keeps the screen on while connected.

## Install
1. On your **phone**, open this repo's **Releases** page and the `latest` release, then tap **PhoneKeys.apk**.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or My Files to "install unknown apps".
3. Open **PhoneKeys** and allow **Nearby devices**.

Requires Android 9 or newer on the phone (any recent Galaxy works).

## First-time pairing
1. In PhoneKeys, tap **Make phone visible** and accept.
2. On the **tablet**, go to *Settings › Connections › Bluetooth* and tap your phone under *Available devices*. Accept the pairing on both screens.
3. Back in PhoneKeys, tap **Connect to tablet** and pick your tablet.
4. Tap the text box on the phone and start typing. The text appears on the tablet wherever its cursor is.

Next time, just open the app and tap **Connect to tablet**.

## Troubleshooting
- **Tablet already paired with the phone (Quick Share, etc.) and it won't connect:** on the tablet, *unpair* the phone. Then pair again while PhoneKeys is open so the tablet sees it as a keyboard.
- **Wrong symbols appear:** on the tablet, go to *Settings › General management › Physical keyboard* and set the layout to **English (US)**.
- **The on-screen keyboard disappears on the tablet:** that's normal when a physical keyboard is connected. Samsung lets you turn the *Show on-screen keyboard* option back on in the same Physical keyboard settings.
- Emoji and accented letters can't be sent; a real US keyboard can't type them either.
- The typing sound plays on the **phone**, not the tablet.
